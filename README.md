## 👋 About Me

I am a **data analytics portfolio builder** focused on SQL-based analysis, business intelligence reporting and communicating operational insights. My public projects explore sales performance, e-commerce, supply chains, logistics, HR, healthcare and customer behavior. I use analytical methods such as joins, CTEs, window functions, DAX and interactive dashboard design **where documented by the individual project**.

**What recruiters can explore:** SQL queries · data models · business problem statements · dashboards · documented findings · recommendations · presentation assets.

> **Transparency:** This portfolio includes practice work, simulated scenarios and case studies. Brand names indicate project themes and do not imply employment or a company partnership. Estimated recommendations are not claimed as measured business outcomes. Some repositories have only dashboard screenshots or learning notes, while others include source files.
